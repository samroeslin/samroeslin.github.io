In October 2017, I joined a reconnaissance team in Mexico City to survey and document building damage following the Puebla earthquake.

This work informed research on post-earthquake building damage assessment and the development of a more consistent approach to collecting building observations.

- [Damage Assessment on Buildings Following the 19th September 2017 Puebla, Mexico Earthquake](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbuil.2018.00072)
- [Final report of the New Zealand reconnaissance team](https://doi.org/10.5459/bnzsee.53.3.150-172)
