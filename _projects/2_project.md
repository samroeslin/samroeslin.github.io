This project applies data science and machine learning to building damage observations collected following the 2017 Puebla earthquake in Mexico City.

The research examines how building characteristics, location, and seismic demand can support damage prediction.

[Read the paper in Earthquake Spectra](https://doi.org/10.1177/8755293020936714).
