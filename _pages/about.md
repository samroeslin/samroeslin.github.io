I work in the [Risk Data Hub (RDH)](https://drmkc.jrc.ec.europa.eu/risk-data-hub) team at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission. My research focuses on disaster risk modelling and the analysis of disaster-related losses and damages, with a particular emphasis on Europe.

My work connects research with policy. I collaborate with European Commission services, including the Directorate-General for European Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid Operations (DG ECHO) and the Directorate-General for Climate Action (DG CLIMA). My collaborations have also included the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA), the European Central Bank (ECB), the World Bank, the Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), and the United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR).

I hold a PhD from the University of Auckland (2022), a master's degree from the Regensburg University of Applied Sciences (2016), bachelor's degrees from the Karlsruhe University of Applied Sciences and the University of Applied Sciences Northwestern Switzerland (2014), and a Licence Professionnelle from the Robert Schuman University Institute of Technology (2014).

My research also draws on practical experience in post-disaster building damage assessment. In October 2017, I joined a reconnaissance mission to Mexico City to survey and document building damage following the Puebla earthquake.
